About

Teresa is an undergraduate at MIT majoring in Artificial Intelligence (6-4) and Mathematics (18). Her research interests lie in generative models and in using them to predict how a scene might unfold when part of it is hidden. She also builds flight hardware, writing radio firmware and designing PCBs for the MIT Rocket Team.

generative models · visionSep 2026 – now

Multi-Future Trajectory Prediction with Conditional Flow Matching

A conditional flow matching model that samples several plausible futures for an occluded obstacle, conditioned on its tracked state history and visibility flags. Training data comes from a 2D vision environment with OpenCV detection and Kalman tracking, split by source trajectory so nothing leaks between train and test.
